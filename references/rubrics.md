# Rubrics

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Use evidence from the candidate's answers, not general impressions.

## Overall Decision

- **Strong hire**: Clear ownership, deep mechanism, strong trade-offs, quantified impact, honest limitations.
- **Hire / lean hire**: Solid execution and reasoning, some gaps but coachable or role-appropriate.
- **No hire / lean no hire**: Vague ownership, shallow mechanisms, fabricated confidence, weak fundamentals, or poor judgment.
- **Not enough signal**: The interview did not reach enough depth. Continue probing.

## Core Dimensions

### Ownership

1. Cannot separate personal work from team work.
2. Names tasks but cannot explain decisions.
3. Owns a defined component with credible detail.
4. Independently drove design, delivery, and trade-offs.
5. Changed direction or standards beyond the immediate task.

### Technical Depth

1. Buzzwords only.
2. Can explain concepts but not implementation.
3. Explains implementation and common edge cases.
4. Handles scale, failure modes, observability, and trade-offs.
5. Reasons from first principles and adapts under new constraints.

### Problem Framing

1. Jumps to solutions without understanding goals.
2. Asks shallow clarifying questions.
3. Identifies goals, constraints, and success metrics.
4. Frames alternatives with costs and risks.
5. Reframes ambiguous problems into high-leverage strategy.

### Execution Judgment

1. Cannot prioritize or sequence work.
2. Relies on others to define next steps.
3. Provides a reasonable plan with risks.
4. Balances speed, quality, cost, and maintainability.
5. Shows strong judgment under uncertainty and pressure.

### Communication

1. Rambling, evasive, or hard to follow.
2. Understandable but unstructured.
3. Clear structure with relevant details.
4. Concise, evidence-based, and responsive to follow-ups.
5. Leads the interviewer through complex reasoning elegantly.

## Red Flags

- Inflates team outcomes into personal ownership.
- Cannot define metrics used in resume claims.
- Avoids failure cases or says nothing went wrong.
- Changes story after follow-up pressure.
- Uses advanced terms without mechanism-level explanation.
- Treats interview as recitation instead of reasoning.
