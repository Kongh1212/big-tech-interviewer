# Follow-Up Ladders

Use these ladders to turn surface answers into interview signal. Pick one path at a time.

## Claim To Evidence

Start when the candidate says they improved, owned, led, optimized, or solved something.

1. What exactly was the original problem?
2. What metric proved it was worth solving?
3. What was the baseline?
4. What changed after your work?
5. How was the metric measured?
6. What else could have caused the change?
7. Which part did you personally do?
8. What decision would have been different without you?

## Project Ownership

1. Why did this project exist?
2. Who proposed the solution?
3. What alternatives were rejected?
4. Which module did you personally design?
5. What was the hardest bug or incident?
6. What part did you not understand at first?
7. What did reviewers challenge?
8. If your tech lead disagreed, how did you defend the choice?

## Technical Depth

1. Explain the core mechanism without buzzwords.
2. Draw the data flow in words.
3. Where are the bottlenecks?
4. What happens at 10x traffic?
5. What happens when a dependency times out?
6. What data can be lost, duplicated, or reordered?
7. How do you observe and alert on it?
8. What would you simplify if rebuilding it?

## Resume Authenticity

1. This line says "responsible for X"; what was your exact deliverable?
2. Which files, services, dashboards, or documents did you own?
3. What would your manager say was your contribution?
4. What did someone else do better than you?
5. Which part would you fail to explain if asked to code it live?
6. What is the most embarrassing detail hidden behind this bullet?

## Behavioral Pressure

1. Tell me about a conflict where you were wrong.
2. What evidence changed your mind?
3. What did you do before escalating?
4. What did the other side reasonably care about?
5. What was the cost of your approach?
6. What would you do differently now?

## Anti-Fluff Phrases

When the candidate uses vague phrasing, interrupt politely and convert it:

- "优化了性能" -> Which metric, baseline, final number, and method?
- "负责架构" -> Which architecture decision did you personally make?
- "提升用户体验" -> Which user behavior changed?
- "高并发" -> What QPS, P99 latency, payload size, and peak pattern?
- "保证稳定性" -> What SLO, incident count, alert, and rollback path?
- "推动落地" -> Who resisted, what trade-off was negotiated, and what shipped?
